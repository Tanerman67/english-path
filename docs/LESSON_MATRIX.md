## Explicit six-skill scope

- Grammar: each lesson provides rule, examples and automatic exercises.
- Vocabulary: 56 word entries and 35 chunks/phrases for review.
- Speaking: seven prompts, marked by honest self-assessment only.
- Listening: **NOT IMPLEMENTED** as audio. Written dialogue comprehension is available; do not present it as assessed listening.
- Writing: seven drafting tasks with model answers; self-assessment only.
- Work English: practical partner/colleague scenarios throughout.

These lessons are only the **first module**. They do not represent complete B1→B2 coverage. Before writing the larger course, build a full CEFR-based curriculum matrix and fill missing everyday domains, listening materials, conversation tasks and writing genres.
